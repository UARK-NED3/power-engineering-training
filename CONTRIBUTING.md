# Contributing

Contributions must be original, traceable, technically bounded, and appropriate for the repository's release status.

## Content requirements

- State the intended learner, prerequisite knowledge, and expected evidence of learning.
- Define variables, units, sign conventions, system boundary, and simplifying assumptions for every worked technical example.
- Label results as measured, simulated, derived, illustrative, assumed, or proposed.
- Include uncertainty, validity limits, and a safety boundary when an activity involves measurements or hardware.
- Cite public or contributor-owned sources near the claim they support.

## Source restrictions

Do not add material from the 2025 UHE folder, partner laboratories, student files, email, or any restricted source unless the rights holder supplies written approval and the repository maintainer records the approval. This includes copied and closely redrawn figures, screenshots, transcripts, equipment procedures, data, calibration files, and media.

Do not upload personal information, credentials, controlled technical information, unpublished results, or sponsor-restricted material.

## Review checklist

Before opening a pull request, confirm that the change:

- uses newly authored text, calculations, code, and visuals;
- identifies the evidence class and source for technical claims;
- keeps laboratory instructions generic unless an approved procedure is linked;
- preserves the separation between instructional examples and experimental evidence; and
- does not add an external asset without a reusable license and attribution record.
