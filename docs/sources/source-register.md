# Source Register

This register records the provenance boundary for curriculum planning. It is not a repository of the referenced materials.

| ID | Source class | Allowed use in this repository | Not allowed | Evidence status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| INT-2025-UHE-ORIENTATION | 2025 UHE program orientation and schedule materials | High-level topic and learning-flow planning | Reproduction of slides, schedules, names, figures, text, or media | Restricted internal planning input |
| INT-2025-UHE-PACKAGING | 2025 power-module packaging instructional materials | Original synthesis of broad concepts such as electrical, thermal, and mechanical package functions | Copying, paraphrasing closely, redrawing figures, or transferring procedures | Restricted internal planning input |
| INT-2025-UHE-LAB | 2025 partner-lab files, updates, equipment tutorials, and laboratory materials | Identification of possible subject areas for a future owner-approved activity | Any redistribution or reuse without owner approval | Restricted internal planning input |
| INT-2026-STUDENT | 2026 application correspondence and student résumé | Determination that onboarding should accommodate an early-career mechanical-engineering student interested in experiments | Inclusion of personal information or source files | Confidential personnel input |

## Interpretation rule

Restricted inputs may shape the curriculum's subject coverage. They may not supply repository assets, wording, procedures, or experimental evidence. Every public-facing item in this repository must stand on its own through original authorship or a separately documented public source.
