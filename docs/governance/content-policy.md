# Content Policy

## Scope

This repository shares original educational material on coupled electrical, thermal, and mechanical behavior in power-electronic systems. It does not function as an archive for another laboratory's training program, equipment documentation, or student records.

## Permitted content

- Original explanatory text, equations, worked examples, code, diagrams, and photographs created for this repository
- Public standards, datasheets, papers, and other sources with an appropriate citation and use rights
- Material supplied by a contributor who confirms ownership or an applicable license
- Partner-laboratory material only after the owner gives explicit written permission and the maintainer records the allowed use

## Prohibited content

- Files or visuals from the 2025 IEEE EPS UHE program or partner laboratories
- Figures recreated from restricted sources closely enough to reproduce their selection, arrangement, or distinctive expression
- Equipment-specific procedures, settings, test data, or design files without owner approval
- Student applications, resumes, emails, grades, identifiers, contact details, or other personal information
- Credentials, export-controlled material, confidential sponsor material, and unpublished research information

## Required provenance record

Every nontrivial imported asset or externally sourced technical claim needs a record containing:

1. source citation or asset URL;
2. rights or license basis;
3. date accessed;
4. permitted use; and
5. whether the repository content is a quotation, summary, calculation, or original interpretation.

Record restricted planning inputs in the source register without placing the source file in this repository.

## Safety boundary

Educational material must distinguish conceptual exercises from approved work. Any hardware activity needs the applicable laboratory's supervision, training, hazard review, written procedure, and stop conditions. Contributors must not infer authorization from the presence of a curriculum topic.
